Why modular

The sensor stack should follow the use case.

01

Reusable base

Compute, wireless, power and firmware foundations can stay consistent while the sensing configuration evolves.

02

Sensor pucks

Replaceable modules create a path toward partner-specific signals without rebuilding the full wearable from zero.

03

Open data access

The platform is designed around controllable data flows and integration into partner-owned systems.

04

White-label direction

Partners can explore OpenPulse as a hardware layer behind their own experience, app or workflow.
