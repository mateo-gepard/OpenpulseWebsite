OpenPulse Gen 2

Thermo+ puck: skin temperature and heat-flux trend

Two skin temperature sensors, one above a defined insulating layer, for a heat-flux trend towards core temperature.

What it measures

Two temperature sensors: one at the skin, one above a defined insulating layer. The difference between them gives a heat-flux trend towards core temperature, next to the skin temperature itself. It is a trend for research, not a clinical thermometer.

Port

Skin side, port A or B. Every port takes any puck; a configuration is simply which puck goes where.

Channels

ChannelSignalRate
skin.temp1Temperature sensor 11 Hz · 16 bit
skin.temp2Temperature sensor 21 Hz · 16 bit

Example configurations

Used in these example configurations: Factory safety, Clinical studies.

Together with the Air puck, air and skin temperature show heat stress before it turns dangerous.

Status

In development. Gen 2 and its pucks are designed and rendered from CAD; they have not yet been built or tested. Values on this page are design targets.

OpenPulse is not a medical device. It is built for research and industrial use, not for diagnosis or treatment.
