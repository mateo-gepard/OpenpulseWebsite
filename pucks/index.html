Sensor pucks for OpenPulse Gen 2

A sensor puck is a small, self-contained sensor module that sits in one of the three ports of the OpenPulse Gen 2 core. Each puck stores its type, serial number and calibration, and that information travels with every recording.

Every port takes any puck. They are swapped in the lab with standard tools, without soldering. And if your application needs a signal we do not cover yet, we build the puck for it.

  • Pulse

    Raw PPG in green, red and infrared with two photodiodes. For pulse, HRV and oxygen saturation research.

  • Thermo+

    Two skin temperature sensors, one above a defined insulating layer, for a heat-flux trend towards core temperature.

  • EKG

    Biopotential front end for single-lead ECG and R-peak timing as a reference for the pulse wave.

  • EDA / BioZ

    Skin conductance, respiration and bioimpedance from 1 to 806 kHz on one front end.

  • Air

    Temperature, humidity, pressure and gas right next to the body. Faces the air, not the skin.
