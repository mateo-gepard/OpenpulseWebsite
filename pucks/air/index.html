OpenPulse Gen 2

Air puck: the environment next to the body

Temperature, humidity, pressure and gas right next to the body. Faces the air, not the skin.

What it measures

The top port faces outward and takes the environment puck your application needs. Its readings share one timeline with pulse, skin and motion: you see how the body reacts, and to what.

  • Factory: the Air puck notices volatile gases rising, for example from a solvent leak, before anyone smells them.
  • Boiler room: air temperature, humidity and skin temperature together show heat stress before it turns dangerous.
  • Mountain: air pressure falling by 4 hPa in three hours means the weather is turning. The same sensor knows the altitude, and the pulse shows how the body copes with both.

Port

Facing the air, port E. Port E faces outward, so this puck reads the air, not the skin.

Example configurations

Used in these example configurations: Research, Factory safety, Clinical studies, Outdoor apps.

Other environment pucks

Port E can take other environment pucks on request, for example for radiation and UV, CO₂, fine dust, ozone, pollen, magnetic field or water contact. We check whether the sensor fits a puck and build it for the same port and the same API.

Status

In development. Gen 2 and its pucks are designed and rendered from CAD; they have not yet been built or tested. Values on this page are design targets.

Not a certified gas detector or dosimeter.
