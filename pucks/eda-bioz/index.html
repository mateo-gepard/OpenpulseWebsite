OpenPulse Gen 2

EDA / BioZ puck: skin conductance and bioimpedance

Skin conductance, respiration and bioimpedance from 1 to 806 kHz on one front end.

What it measures

Electrodermal activity (skin conductance), respiration and bioimpedance from 1 to 806 kHz, all on one front end. Skin conductance together with pulse is a common signal pair in stress research. Research use only; no health statements about body composition or hydration.

Port

Skin side, port A or B. Every skin-side port takes any body puck, and two pucks of the same type can run at the same time.

Channels

Channel names and sample rates for the EDA / BioZ puck will be published with the API.

Example configurations

Used in these example configurations: Trading desks.

Example pilot from our process: a trading desk, 5 traders, 14 days, then a plan for 40 devices with the same configuration.

Status

In development. Gen 2 and its pucks are designed and rendered from CAD; they have not yet been built or tested. Values on this page are design targets.

OpenPulse is not a medical device. It is built for research and industrial use, not for diagnosis or treatment.
