OpenPulse Gen 2

Pulse puck: raw PPG

Raw PPG in green, red and infrared with two photodiodes. For pulse, HRV and oxygen saturation research.

What it measures

Three light colours (green, red and infrared) and two photodiodes give six raw PPG channels. You get the raw light signal, not a heart-rate score, so pulse, heart-rate variability and oxygen saturation can be derived with your own methods.

Port

Skin side, port A or B. Every port takes any puck; a configuration is simply which puck goes where.

Channels

ChannelSignalRate
ppg.green.pd1Green light, photodiode 1100 Hz · 24 bit
ppg.green.pd2Green light, photodiode 2100 Hz · 24 bit
ppg.red.pd1Red light, photodiode 1100 Hz · 24 bit
ppg.red.pd2Red light, photodiode 2100 Hz · 24 bit
ppg.ir.pd1Infrared light, photodiode 1100 Hz · 24 bit
ppg.ir.pd2Infrared light, photodiode 2100 Hz · 24 bit

Example configurations

Used in these example configurations: Research, Factory safety, Trading desks, Sports science, Clinical studies, Outdoor apps.

Status

In development. Gen 2 and its pucks are designed and rendered from CAD; they have not yet been built or tested. Values on this page are design targets.

OpenPulse is not a medical device. It is built for research and industrial use, not for diagnosis or treatment.
