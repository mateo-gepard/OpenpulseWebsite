OpenPulse Gen 2

EKG puck: single-lead ECG

Biopotential front end for single-lead ECG and R-peak timing as a reference for the pulse wave.

What it measures

A biopotential front end for a single-lead ECG. Its R-peaks give an exact timing reference for the pulse wave measured by the Pulse puck in the other skin port. For research use only; it makes no rhythm or diagnostic statements.

Port

Skin side, port A or B. Every skin-side port takes any body puck, and two pucks of the same type can run at the same time.

Channels

Channel names and sample rates for the EKG puck will be published with the API.

Example configurations

Used in these example configurations: Research, Sports science.

Status

In development. Gen 2 and its pucks are designed and rendered from CAD; they have not yet been built or tested. Values on this page are design targets.

OpenPulse is not a medical device. It is built for research and industrial use, not for diagnosis or treatment.
