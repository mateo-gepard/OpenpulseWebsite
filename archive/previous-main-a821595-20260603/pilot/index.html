What happens next

A serious pilot conversation has a process.

OpenPulse is still early-stage hardware. The goal is to find use cases where configurable sensing and partner-controlled data are valuable enough to justify prototype validation.

01

Use-case review

We map the workflow, buyer, users, signals, constraints and why fixed wearables are not enough.

Decision: real need or nice demo?
02

Sensor/data brief

We define which signals, sampling assumptions, export path and dashboard/API needs matter for the partner.

Decision: feasible direction?
03

Prototype validation

We validate puck assembly, contact, electrical behavior and signal quality before any product claims.

Decision: pilot-grade evidence?
04

Pilot scope

If the evidence is strong enough, the next step is a focused pilot with clear success criteria.

Decision: build together?
Prototype disclaimer

Ambitious does not mean finished.

The main PCB works. Sensor puck PCBs and components exist, but the puck system still needs assembly, reflow, electrical bring-up, mechanical testing and signal validation. OpenPulse is not a medical device or a validated safety product.
