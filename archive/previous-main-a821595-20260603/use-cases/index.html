Good pilot criteria

The best first market has a specific pain, budget and data need.

Clear buyerSomeone owns the problem and can sponsor a pilot.
Measurable workflowThe sensor data connects to a real operational or product experience.
Configuration needA fixed consumer wearable is not flexible enough for the use case.
Responsible data modelThe partner understands that biosensor data needs careful handling.
