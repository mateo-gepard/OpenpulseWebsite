Modular B2B wearable platform

One wearable. The sensors your company needs.

OpenPulse is a modular B2B wearable sensor platform. Companies select the 10 mm sensor pucks required for their use case; we configure the hardware and software, provide access to the underlying raw sensor data, and integrate it directly into their existing systems.

Why OpenPulse

Fixed devices force companies to compromise.

Most wearables use fixed sensor combinations and expose only predefined metrics. When a company needs different sensors or direct integration into its systems, it often has to compromise or develop a new wearable from scratch.

The platform

Reuse the core. Change the sensing.

OpenPulse keeps one core device and changes the sensor configuration and data path for each company’s use case.
