OpenPulse Gen 2

About OpenPulse

OpenPulse is a student founding team based in Munich, Germany. We are developing OpenPulse Gen 2, a modular research wearable with swappable sensor pucks that gives research and industry teams the raw data, on their own computers.

What we build

One core, swappable sensor pucks and a configuration built around each application: the platform, the sensor pucks and the data path. In development. Gen 2 and its pucks are designed and rendered from CAD; they have not yet been built or tested. Values on this page are design targets.

Name

OpenPulse (openpulse.eu) is a modular research wearable. It is not related to the OpenQASM "OpenPulse" pulse grammar, to openpulse.org or to other projects with the same name.
