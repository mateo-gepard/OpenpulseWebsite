OpenPulse Gen 2

Use cases: one configuration per application

With OpenPulse Gen 2, a configuration is simply which sensor puck goes in which port. Your team fits the pucks in the lab before the study or rollout starts. These are the example configurations shown on our homepage.

Research

Port A: Pulse · Port B: EKG · Port E: Air

Raw PPG next to a single-lead ECG: the ECG R-peaks give a timing reference for the pulse wave. The Air puck records the conditions around each participant on the same timeline.

Factory safety

Port A: Thermo+ · Port B: Pulse · Port E: Air

Air temperature, humidity and skin temperature together show heat stress before it turns dangerous. The Air puck notices volatile gases rising, for example from a solvent leak, before anyone smells them. Not a certified gas detector.

Trading desks

Port A: EDA / BioZ · Port B: Pulse · Port E: free

Skin conductance and pulse for stress research at the desk. Example pilot from our process: 5 traders, 14 days, then a plan for 40 devices with the same configuration.

Sports science

Port A: Pulse · Port B: EKG · Port E: free

Raw PPG and single-lead ECG side by side, with motion from the core IMU at 50 Hz, so pulse-wave and heart-rate methods can be compared on the same recording.

Clinical studies

Port A: Pulse · Port B: Thermo+ · Port E: Air

Raw pulse, skin temperature with a heat-flux trend, and the surrounding air. For research studies only: OpenPulse is not a medical device and makes no diagnostic statements.

Outdoor apps

Port A: Pulse · Port B: free · Port E: Air

Air pressure falling by 4 hPa in three hours means the weather is turning. The same sensor knows the altitude, and the pulse shows how the body copes with both.

How we work together

  1. Talk. Tell us what you want to measure, on whom and for how long. You get a proposal in writing.
  2. Configure. Pick a puck for each port. Any port can change, any time.
  3. Pilot. A few devices on real people first. We do the work; your team hands out devices and docks them.

All configurations are examples. In development. Gen 2 and its pucks are designed and rendered from CAD; they have not yet been built or tested. Values on this page are design targets.
