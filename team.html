Team

Three founders. One open system.

OpenPulse started because Mateo was frustrated with a subscription wearable. It became a modular biosignal platform once the team realized the sharper problem was B2B: give partners exactly the sensors and data path they need.

Origin story

From "no subscription" to a configurable platform.

The first idea was an open-source fitness band: privacy-friendly, repairable, and customizable. The strategic shift came after hard target-group conversations: "everyone" was not a market. Pilot partners were.
